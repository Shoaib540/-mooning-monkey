Target: Instagram boutiques, salons, clinics, tutors, home bakers, mobile shops.

Rule: personalize first line with their page name. Max 20/day. No spam blasts.

---

### Script A — boutique / Instagram seller

Assalam o Alaikum {Name}!
{Page} pe orders WhatsApp pe aate hon ge — kabhi reply late ho jata hoga.
Main same-day **WhatsApp Reply System** set karta hoon (FAQ + price + order flow) — **Rs 12,000**.
Agar 2-min call ok ho to time bata den.

### Script B — salon / clinic

Assalam o Alaikum! {Business} ke liye booking + timing FAQs ka ready WhatsApp pack banata hoon taake aap busy hon to bhi lead drop na ho. Rs 12,000, 48 hrs. Demo chahiye?

### Script C — ultra short

{Name}, Instagram/WhatsApp sellers ke liye same-day listing + reply packs karta hoon (Rs 5k–12k). 1 example bhejoun?

### Script D — after they say interest

Shukriya. 50% JazzCash/Easypaisa pe advance, baqi delivery pe.
Bhej den: (1) business 3 lines (2) top 8 customer questions (3) price list.
Kal / aaj delivery.

### Follow-up (36 hrs, no reply)

Bas soft check — pack chahiye tha ya skip karun?
