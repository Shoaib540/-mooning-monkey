# Delivery SOP — forward client brief to Cursor

## Listing Pack

**Ask client:**
- Niche, language, 10 products (name, price, 3 features each)

**Deliver (Markdown or Google Doc):**
1. Title options (2 each)
2. 5 bullets
3. WhatsApp pitch (2–3 lines)
4. Hashtag line (optional)

**Timebox:** 45–90 minutes with AI assist.

---

## WhatsApp Reply System

**Ask client:**
- Business name, hours, area, payment methods
- Top 8 FAQs
- Order steps (COD? advance?)
- Tone: formal / friendly Roman Urdu

**Deliver:**
1. `quick-replies.txt` — labeled shortcuts
2. `faq-ur-en.md` — full answers
3. `handoff.md` — when human must take over
4. 5-min setup screenshots checklist for WhatsApp Business app

---

## AI Inbox Setup

**Ask client:**
- Preferred tool (ManyChat / n8n / Make)
- WhatsApp number type (Business app vs Cloud API)
- Google Sheet access

**Deliver:**
- Flow map PDF/MD
- Working automation + test chat log
- Loom or step list to pause/edit
- 7-day bugfix window
